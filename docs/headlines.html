Do different kinds of posts help?

Fixed keyword buckets: events → promotion → technical → other. For each kind, add its count and mean sentiment to market history. All comparisons use the full test panel; observed-kind counts show how sparse the evidence is. Categories are approximate rules, not manually validated labels.

What would this justify?

It justifies questioning claims of reliable sentiment-based forecasting. It does not justify buying, selling, shorting, leverage, profit expectations, or claims that tweets cause prices to change. Nor does this result prove that all prediction is impossible.

The final period was inspected in earlier work; this is an exploratory extension, not an untouched confirmatory test. Pretraining overlap, collection timestamps, edits and sparse archive coverage limit the inference. No fees, slippage or execution study was performed.
