What does this post predict?

Paste a public X post, select its memecoin, and run the trained next-day forecast.

No demonstrated trading edge. Our tweet-only model scored 49.28% versus 51.60% for the training-majority rule on the matched test set. Estimates are not causal tweet impacts or financial advice.

No live X feed is connected. Text is processed in your browser and is not uploaded.

How it is trained

Character TF-IDF learns wording from earlier posts; logistic regression learns actual next-UTC-day price direction. A separate ridge regression estimates whole-coin return. Optional recent context uses strictly earlier posts. Vocabulary and weights are fitted on training dates only; regularisation is selected on validation dates. The final market dates are held out from training but previously explored, so results are exploratory.

Horizon: the next complete UTC calendar day, open to close—not the next 24 hours and not the price change caused by a post.
