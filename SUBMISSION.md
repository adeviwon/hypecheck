# HypeCheck submission

**AI + Finance · Malvern Little Geniuses**

Nathaniel Wong (model), Aaden Yick (data), Alastair Chu (demo)

HypeCheck tests whether the wording and estimated sentiment of public memecoin posts improve next-day direction forecasts. The notebook scores user-supplied posts and compares the forecast with simpler baselines. The retrospective tests found no advantage over coin identity alone.
